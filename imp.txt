=========================================
      QUIZIFY - CLASSROOM EDITION
=========================================

Quizify is now a classroom-based system where Teachers manage quizzes
and Students participate in their assigned rooms.

--- HOW TO RUN THE PROJECT ---

1. Prerequisites:
   - Python 3.x installed.
   - Run: pip install flask flask-sqlalchemy flask-login werkzeug

2. Start the Server:
   - Run: python run.py

3. Access the App:
   - Go to: http://127.0.0.1:5000

--- CLASSROOM WORKFLOW ---

FOR TEACHERS (Admins):
1. REGISTER: Choose the 'Teacher' role during signup.
2. CREATE ROOM: Go to 'Classrooms' -> 'Create Classroom'.
3. SHARE CODE: Each classroom has a unique 'Join Code'. Give this to your students.
4. MANAGE QUIZZES: Enter the classroom and click 'Create New Quiz'. 
   These quizzes are private to your classroom members.

FOR STUDENTS:
1. REGISTER: Choose the 'Student' role during signup.
2. JOIN ROOM: Go to 'Classrooms' -> 'Join Classroom' and enter the code
   provided by your teacher.
3. TAKE QUIZZES: Once joined, enter the classroom to see and take 
   available quizzes.

--- GENERAL FEATURES ---

- DETAILED RESULTS: See your score and a breakdown of correct/incorrect 
  answers immediately after submission.
- PROFILE: Update your name and credentials in the 'Account' section.
- ROBUSTNESS: Explicit checks for duplicate usernames/emails to 
  prevent errors.

Enjoy the new Classroom experience!
